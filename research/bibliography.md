# Finkelstein and Mearsheimer: works

Compiled 2026-11-10 from Wikipedia's book lists (checked against publisher pages where noted). For the rhetoric study,
what matters is which claims from these works show up on stream or in chat. Each "Claims to track" line says what the
watchlist codes. Full texts are not stored here: the books are in copyright. Get them from a library or the publisher;
articles the authors host themselves are linked.

Key articles (Ukraine/Russia unless noted):
- "Why the Ukraine Crisis Is the West's Fault: The Liberal Delusions That Provoked Putin", *Foreign Affairs*, Sep/Oct 2014 (`west_fault`).
- "Faulty Powers: Who Started the Ukraine Crisis?", *Foreign Affairs*, Nov/Dec 2014: exchange with McFaul and Sestanovich, and his reply.
- "Getting Ukraine Wrong", *New York Times*, Mar 2014; "Don't Arm Ukraine", *New York Times*, Feb 2015.
- "The Causes and Consequences of the Ukraine War", *Horizons*, 2022 (from a lecture; answered by Alexander Stubb).
- "Playing With Fire in Ukraine: The Underappreciated Risks of Catastrophic Escalation", *Foreign Affairs*, Aug 2022.
- *New Yorker* interview with Isaac Chotiner, 2022 ("no evidence that he had imperial ambitions"); *Oxford Political
  Review* interview, Jan 2023 (`putin_not_imperial`).
- "The Israel Lobby", *London Review of Books*, Mar 2006, with Walt (the essay before the book).
- "Bound to Fail: The Rise and Fall of the Liberal International Order", *International Security*, 2019.
- China: "Can China Rise Peacefully?" (*The National Interest*, 2014), "The Inevitable Rivalry" (*Foreign Affairs*, 2021).

His CV with the full publication list, and PDFs of many articles, are on mearsheimer.com.
