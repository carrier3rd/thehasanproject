## Claude's predictions
1. **Explicit tier is rare in chat:** endorsed explicit-tier antisemitism (slurs, Nazi codes, denial terms, calls for
   violence) is under 0.1% of chat messages in every stream.
2. **Definitions diverge:** among chat messages about Israel or Jews, the IHRA count is at least 5x the JDA count.
3. **Concentration:** the top 10% of accounts with any endorsed antisemitism or Holocaust-distortion flag write at
   least 40% of those messages.
4. **Moderation works but leaks:** in the live log, deleted messages carry at least 5x the flag rate of kept messages,
   and at least one endorsed explicit-tier message per stream-day stays up.
5. **Chat follows the stream:** chat's Israel/Palestine topic rate is at least 3x higher during transcript windows where
   Hasan is on that topic than during other windows.
6. **Hasan, explicit tier:** zero endorsed explicit-tier instances in Hasan's own speech across the 5 VODs.
7. **Hasan, contested tier:** in any VOD where Israel/Palestine takes more than 10% of stream time, at least one endorsed
   contested-tier instance (Israel-Nazi comparison, Holocaust inversion or similar).
8. **Hasan, US targets:** at least 60% of Hasan's negative US segments are aimed at agencies, companies or the system
   rather than a named administration (socialscope pilot: about 19 of 23).
9. **Russia:** zero endorsed false Kremlin narratives (`kremlin_narratives.csv`) by Hasan; "West's fault"/NATO framing
   in at most 2 segments across the 5 VODs.
10. **Keywords miss things:** in the random 500-message samples, Claude finds endorsed flags the patterns missed at a
    rate of at least 0.2% of messages, so keyword-only counts are an undercount.
11. **Coder reliability:** Claude and the owner agree on stance for at least 80% of the 100 hand-checked items.
