About this archive

This is an unofficial, searchable archive of one streamer's past broadcasts. Each stream has a machine-generated transcript with speaker labels, and the chat messages sent while it aired, lined up against the video. The video itself is not hosted here: every page embeds or links to the original.

Transcripts and speaker labels are produced by speech-recognition software and contain mistakes. Names are attached only to a small set of public figures; everyone else appears as "Guest", and audio from videos being watched on stream appears as "Media". Sections muted on the original video are left out.

Chat messages and opting out

Chat is shown with the username it was sent under. Messages removed by moderators are shown as a placeholder rather than their text. If you would like your messages removed from this site, write to the address below from any account or address, stating your Twitch username. Your messages, under your current and previous names, are excluded the next time the site is rebuilt, normally within a day.
