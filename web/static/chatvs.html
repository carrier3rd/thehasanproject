How moments are found

Every minute of chat is scored for pushback words ("wrong", "not true", "cap", "bad take", "????" and so on), against that stream's usual level, and for bursts of messages deleted by moderators. Claude (AI) then reads the transcript and the chat around the strongest minutes and keeps the ones where chat was really reacting to what Hasan said. Example messages are copied exactly; chat usernames aren't shown, and anything the site's chat check flagged as hateful is never used as an example.
