How moments are found

Every minute of chat is scored against that stream's usual level for pushback words ("wrong", "not true", "bad take", "????"), identity-politics language ("problematic", "do better", "transphobic") and hostility toward outsiders ("libs", "grifter", "why is he here"), the last only while Hasan is talking about someone outside the left such as The Bulwark's hosts. Stretches the stream index marks as games or his own life are skipped. Claude (AI) then reads the transcript and the chat around the strongest minutes and keeps the ones where chat was really reacting to what Hasan said. Example messages are copied exactly; chat usernames aren't shown, and anything the site's chat check flagged as hateful is never used as an example.
