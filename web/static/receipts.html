How this works

Where he stood: Claude (AI) reads each stream's transcript and notes the positions Hasan states on each story, in one plain sentence, next to his exact words copied from the transcript and a link to the moment. Nothing is judged here; the sentence is Claude's summary and the quote is what he said.

Then vs now is stricter:

  1. Claude looks across weeks for pairs of his positions that conflict on the same question. Jokes, bits, reading something out, conditional statements and changes that follow new facts don't count.
  2. A second, stricter check reads the transcript around both quotes and rejects anything that falls apart in context.
  3. A person reads each one that passes and approves it or throws it out. Only approved pairs appear.

Transcripts are machine-made and can mishear words. The linked video is the authoritative record. Think something is unfair? Use the contact on the About page.
