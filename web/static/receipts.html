How a receipt gets here
  1. Claude (AI) lists the positions Hasan states on each story, copying his exact words from the transcript.
  2. It looks across weeks for pairs that conflict on the same question. Jokes, bits, reading something out, conditional statements and changes that follow new facts don't count.
  3. A second, stricter check reads the transcript around both quotes and rejects anything that falls apart in context.
  4. A person reads each one that passes and approves it or throws it out. Only approved receipts appear here.

Transcripts are machine-made and can mishear words. The linked video is the authoritative record. Think one is unfair? Use the contact on the About page.
