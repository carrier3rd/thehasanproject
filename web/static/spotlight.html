How this works

Claude (AI) reads each stream's machine transcript and notes the stories Hasan spent real time on, roughly how long, and what he said about them, copying his exact words. Stories are then matched to Reddit by a few specific keywords per story (listed on each story page). The Reddit numbers count posts and comments that mention those keywords, as a share of all posts and comments in that community over the same days. Moderator-removed posts are left out. Reddit usernames are never stored or shown.

The Reddit data covers some weeks and not others. Days without data are hatched in the charts and never counted as zero. Blindspot means Reddit discussed it a lot on days Hasan streamed, while he spent under 3 minutes on it. Stream focus means an hour or more on stream on days the subreddits barely mentioned it. Groupings of communities are only a reading aid; every subreddit is listed by name on the story page. Times are estimates.
